# SetForge — 6-Minute Solo Athlete Screencast

**Format:** voiceover + screen recording.
**Runtime:** ~6 minutes (target 950–1,100 words at 160–170 wpm).
**Audience:** new solo swimmer, signed up but hasn't generated their first workout yet.
**Goal:** by the end, the viewer knows how to set their pace, generate a workout, read it on deck, tell SetForge what they like and don't like, and log it when they're done.

Voiceover prose is plain text. `[SCREEN ACTION]` and `[B-ROLL/CAPTION]` are bracketed cues for the editor.

Named features the viewer will see (capped at 5): pace, generate, regenerate, ★/👎, Run + log.

---

## 0. Opening hook (~25 sec, ~70 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: 2-sec slow zoom on the dark-navy SetForge landing page with the "Sign in with Apple" button visible. Subtle ambient pool sound underneath.]

You already know how to swim. What you probably don't have is a coach who writes you a fresh workout every time you show up to the pool. That's what SetForge does. Six minutes from now, you'll have your first workout generated, you'll know how to read it on deck, you'll know how to teach SetForge what you like, and you'll know how to log it. Let's get into it.

[CAPTION: "SetForge — your first generated workout"]

---

## 1. Sign in (~45 sec, ~120 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: tap "Sign in with Apple". Apple consent sheet appears, blur the email field. Land on the generator page.]

SetForge is invite-only. If you're watching this, someone sent you an invite link — that's how you get an account. Tap the link, then tap **Sign in with Apple**. SetForge never asks for a password, never stores one, and only gets the anonymized Apple ID it needs to keep your workouts and settings tied to you. Sign in on a phone today, your laptop tomorrow — your history follows you.

[B-ROLL: small overlay of the URL bar showing https://setforge.io.]

[CAPTION: "Sign in with Apple — invite-only, no passwords ever."]

Once you're signed in, you're looking at the generator page. This is your home base.

---

## 2. Tell SetForge how fast you swim (~50 sec, ~135 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: hover the pace input field above the yardage slider. Type 2:00. The slider's time-preset buttons (~60 min, ~90 min, ~2hr) tick to fresh values.]

The single most important thing you'll set today is your **per-100 pace**. This is the time you can comfortably hold for 100 yards over a longer set — not your best 100, your honest cruise. Type it as minutes-colon-seconds. Two-oh-oh for two minutes flat. One-forty for a competitive swimmer. Two-thirty if you're easing back in.

[CAPTION: "Per-100 pace = honest cruise, not best effort."]

[SCREEN ACTION: in the header tap 👤, then in Profile hover **🎯 Swimmer level** and the three pills.]

If you genuinely don't know your pace yet, tap **🎯 Swimmer level** in your profile and pick Recreational, Masters, or Competitive. That sets a reasonable starting pace. The workbook that goes with this video has a Test Set protocol — swim a timed 200, divide by two, you're done.

---

## 3. Pick a type and generate (~65 sec, ~175 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: cursor pans across the nine workout-type cards. Pause on each for a beat. Then click **IM**.]

Nine workout types. **IM** mixes all four strokes. **Distance** is sustained aerobic. **Sprint** is short, hard, with rest. **Endurance** sits between distance and sprint. **Technique** is drill-heavy. **Mixed** rotates kick, pull, swim. The last three — **Backstroke**, **Breaststroke**, **Butterfly** — are stroke specialties.

[CAPTION: "Nine types. Pick the one that matches what your body needs today."]

[SCREEN ACTION: drag the yardage slider to ~2400. Tap the ~60 min preset to show how it moves.]

Drag the slider to set your target distance. Or tap a time preset — sixty, ninety, or two hours — and the slider figures out the yardage from your pace.

[SCREEN ACTION: cycle each equipment icon (kickboard, fins, paddles, pull buoy, snorkel) through off → preferred → required. Settle on Fins=preferred.]

Tap an equipment card to cycle it. Off means it's not considered. **Preferred** nudges the generator toward sets that use it. **Required** is a hard filter — every set will include it.

[SCREEN ACTION: tap **🏊 Generate Workout**. A 2,400yd IM workout appears with four colored blocks: Warm-Up, Drill, Main, Cool-Down.]

Hit **Generate Workout**. Four blocks. Color-coded. Total yardage and estimated time up top.

---

## 4. Read it on deck and customize (~70 sec, ~190 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: scroll slowly through the four blocks. Each block expands to show sets: reps × distance, description, interval.]

Every block has the same shape: a name, total yardage, and a list of sets. Each set is **reps times distance**, a **description** of what to swim, and an **interval** in the rightmost column — that's the time you leave the wall on.

[CAPTION: "Reps × Distance · Description · Interval"]

[SCREEN ACTION: hover the small effort-zone pill in a block header (e.g. ● THRESHOLD in yellow).]

The colored pill on each block tells you the dominant effort — easy, aerobic, threshold, VO2, or anaerobic. Use it to pace yourself across the workout.

[SCREEN ACTION: tap the ↺ icon on the Drill block. A new drill set replaces it. Cursor briefly hovers the ⇄ swap icon on a single set inside the Main block.]

Don't love what you got? Each block has a **↺ regenerate** button that swaps just that block. Inside a block, the **⇄ swap** icon next to a set offers a shorter or longer version. The other three blocks stay put.

[SCREEN ACTION: tap the **🎯 Focus** field above the blocks and type "smooth turns."]

The **🎯 Focus** field lets you set a one-line intention. It shows up on the print sheet and during Run Mode between sets.

---

## 5. Teach it what you like and don't like (~55 sec, ~145 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: cursor moves to the Main block header. A three-segment toggle is visible: ★ Favorite / — Neutral / 👎 Disfavor. Hover each segment.]

Look at the top of the main set block. Three buttons: **star, dash, thumbs-down**. This is how you teach SetForge what to give you more of and what to give you less of.

[SCREEN ACTION: click ★ on the main block. Brief flash. Then move to a single set row inside the block. The leading button shows ☆; tap it once to see ★, tap again to see 👎.]

Star a main set you liked — SetForge boosts it three-times in the picker the next time you generate. Thumbs-down one you didn't — it drops to a quarter weight, so you'll see it rarely. The same button shows up on individual set rows too: tap once to favorite that specific set, again to disfavor, again to clear.

[CAPTION: "★ = 3× more often. 👎 = ¼ as often. Use both. Change your mind anytime."]

Open **👤 Profile** anytime to see everything you've curated, with a one-tap Remove on each row.

---

## 6. Run it, log it (~75 sec, ~200 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: tap **🖨 Print Workout**. The browser print preview opens — a clean black-and-white layout. Cancel out.]

Two ways to actually use the workout in the pool. **Print** gives you an 8.5-by-11 page you can fold in a Ziploc bag and put on the deck.

[SCREEN ACTION: tap **▶ Run**. Rest-picker dialog appears. Select **10s**, leave **🔔 Audio cues** on, tap **▶ Start**.]

Or tap **▶ Run** for Run Mode. It walks you through the workout one set at a time on your phone. Pick a rest mode — manual if you control the pace, or a countdown timer that auto-advances. Keep audio cues on; you'll hear three beeps as the rest ends and a higher tone when it's time to push off.

[SCREEN ACTION: rotate phone preview to landscape. The pace clock fills the screen — big countdown, elapsed time top-right, set details below.]

Rotate the phone landscape for the full-screen pace clock. Big countdown. Tap **✓ Lap** every time you touch the wall — SetForge captures your actual split versus the expected interval.

[SCREEN ACTION: jump to the workout-finish screen. Tap **"Log as today's session"**. Set a 4-star difficulty rating.]

When you finish, SetForge offers a one-tap **"Log as today's session"** button. Add a difficulty rating if you want. Done. The workout — splits, focus, everything — is now in your history.

---

## 7. Closing CTA (~25 sec, ~70 words)

[SCREEN ACTION: brief montage — generator page, then history view with a few logged workouts and a yards-per-week sparkline.]

That's the loop. Set your pace once. Generate something. Read it. Star what you liked. Swim it. Log it. The more you log, the smarter SetForge gets about what you actually want — which workouts you've done lately, which ones you've starred, where your week stands against your goal.

[CAPTION: "Generate your first workout this week."]

Generate your first one this week. I'll see you on deck.

[B-ROLL: 1-second fade to the SetForge logo on a dark-navy background. Cut.]

## Production notes

- Record voiceover dry. The pool ambient under the opening hook should drop out by 0:08; the rest of the script is voice + screen UI only.
- Capture the screen recording at 60fps so cursor motion is legible — the tri-state toggle in Section 5 is small; consider a brief zoom-in (1.2×) on that block header for the click sequence.
- Do not show real Apple ID emails or personal data in the consent sheet — blur on capture.
- Default SetForge to a fresh account state for the recording (empty Quick-Launch, no Focus note carried over). For Section 5 specifically, choose a generated workout where the Main block has a clean named label — easier to read what's being starred.
- The brand is **SetForge** (capital S AND F). Verify any on-screen overlays / captions match.
