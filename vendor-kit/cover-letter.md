## What's in the bundle

- **Services Agreement** — the contract. Annual Program-tier billing
  ($300/yr) with auto-renew, terminable with 60-day notice. Includes the
  free-tier permanence clause: swimmers on the free tier stay free forever
  by ToS, regardless of paid-tier business outcomes.
- **Data Processing Addendum (DPA)** — covers personal data handling,
  retention, deletion, and the rights flowing through to your swimmers.
- **Breach Notification SLA** — 24 hours from confirmed breach to written
  notice. Honest about the SLA: SetForge is operated by one person; a
  4-hour or 1-hour commitment would be a lie.
- **Continuity Commitment** — what happens if I'm hit by a bus. 90-day
  notice, JSON data export, migration scripts, and an open-source release
  of the codebase under MIT license. Same text as our ToS section on
  continuity; reproduced here as a procurement-friendly standalone.
- **Sub-processor List** — every vendor that touches your data, what
  they see, and where they're located. Mirrors the public list at
  `https://setforge.io/sub-processors.html`.
- **W-9 form** — for your accounts-payable file.

## What's NOT in the bundle (in the interest of honesty)

A board treasurer reviewing a vendor often expects these. SetForge does
not have them, and won't pretend otherwise:

- **No SOC 2 attestation.** SetForge is too small to justify the audit cost.
- **No annual pen-test report.** Same reason.
- **No HIPAA Business Associate Agreement.** SetForge is not a HIPAA-covered
  service; we don't accept PHI and the ToS disclaims it.
- **No self-serve JSON export portal yet.** Data export today is by request
  via email (`hello@competitionaquatics.com`). A self-serve export portal is on the
  roadmap; the DPA's data-rights language reflects current state honestly.
- **No cyber-insurance certificate.** Available if your board specifically
  requires one — I'll purchase a policy and add the certificate to this
  kit when first asked.

I'd rather you know what you're getting + what you aren't, upfront, than
discover a gap mid-engagement.

## A note on solo-operator SaaS

SetForge is built and operated by one person — me. That changes the
shape of vendor commitments compared to a venture-backed SaaS:

- I'm the one who answers `hello@competitionaquatics.com`. There's no support tier
  to escalate through. Mean response time is < 24 hours.
- The continuity commitment is real because I wrote the open-source-on-
  shutdown clause into the ToS before any pilot signed. If something
  happens to me, the codebase becomes MIT-licensed and your data gets
  exported.
- The free tier stays free forever. That's not marketing — it's contract
  language in §X of the Services Agreement.
