Security at SetForge

A buyer-facing summary of how SetForge handles authentication, data storage, and privacy. For the full legal text, see the Privacy Policy.

No analytics No ad pixels No password storage OAuth-only auth Audit log on every mutation Self-serve data export (planned) SOC 2: not pursued

Authentication

OAuth-only. SetForge never stores a password because we never have one. Sign-in is delegated to your existing identity provider:

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple (live today, primary method)
  • Google OAuth (planned; see changelog)

SetForge stores the opaque sub identifier returned by the provider plus the email address you authorized it to share. No third-party trackers, no analytics SDKs, no behavioral fingerprinting.

What we collect

Only what's needed to operate the app:

  • Account: email, OAuth provider sub, display name, optional DOB (collected once for minor-protection rules, derived live as is_minor; never cached)
  • Workouts: the workouts you generate, your favorites/disfavorites, your settings (pace, equipment, training phase)
  • Assignments + attendance: if your coach assigns workouts to you, and whether you marked them complete
  • Audit log: every mutation to your account or relationships is logged with timestamp + actor; logs survive account deletion in anonymized form (sub NULLed)

What we do NOT collect

  • Behavioral analytics events (no Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, Plausible, Fathom, etc.)
  • Session recordings (no FullStory, Hotjar, LogRocket)
  • Ad pixels (no Facebook Pixel, LinkedIn Insight, Twitter pixel)
  • A/B testing data (no LaunchDarkly, Optimizely, Statsig)
  • Marketing tracking from email (planned email won't carry tracking pixels either)

See the sub-processor list for every vendor that touches user data.

Data storage

  • Region: United States. Hosted on Spaceship Hyperlift.
  • At rest: standard provider encryption (database + filesystem).
  • In transit: HTTPS only. HSTS enabled.
  • Backups: per Spaceship's backup retention policy. We don't operate a separate off-site backup tier today (called out honestly; see "What we don't have" below).

Minor-athlete safety posture

SetForge is built knowing minors will use it (as swimmers under coach-managed accounts). Current protections:

  • Under-13 claim blocked. A coach-managed swimmer record cannot be claimed by a swimmer until they are 13+.
  • Parent-managed flag. Coaches can mark a swimmer record as parent-managed, which blocks the claim flow even at 13+ unless a parent unlocks it.
  • Roster-visibility default-off when minors present. Groups containing under-18 swimmers force minor-protection defaults.
  • Discord feedback bypass for minors. Feedback submitted by accounts under 18 never leaves SetForge servers; it stays in the in-app admin queue.
  • Full MAAP/SafeSport pack: planned but not yet shipped. See What's Coming.

Your rights

  • Data export: by request today; self-serve JSON export is on the roadmap for shipment with the Program tier.
  • Account deletion: available today by request. Cascades to your workouts, favorites, and settings. Audit logs survive with your sub NULLed.
  • Correction: most personal data fields are editable in-app via your Profile.
  • Sub-processor changes: announced on the sub-processor list + changelog.

Continuity commitment

SetForge is operated by a single founder. The single-operator continuity risk is real and we address it explicitly. From our Terms of Service, if SetForge ever ceases operation:

  • 90-day advance notice by email to all active users
  • Self-serve data export in JSON throughout the notice period
  • Published migration scripts so any third party can self-host
  • Open-source the SetForge codebase at the start of the notice period

This is the strongest continuity commitment a solo-operator SaaS can credibly make. See Pricing for the detailed breakdown.

Vendor paper kit

For 501(c)(3) boards and school program reviewers, SetForge ships a procurement-grade vendor paper kit on request. It includes the signed services agreement, Data Processing Addendum, breach-notification SLA (24 hours), continuity commitment as a standalone PDF, sub-processor list, and the W-9 with Competition Aquatics, LLC's EIN. The cover letter is honest about what we DON'T have (SOC 2, pen-test, HIPAA BAA — see below).

To request the kit, email hello@setforge.io with your organization name and treasurer contact. Kit is sent as email attachments — no self-serve portal in v1 (one-touch process by the operator, who is also the one answering your follow-up questions).

What we don't have (honest list)

For symmetry — things buyers might expect from a larger SaaS that SetForge does NOT have today:

  • SOC 2 Type II. Not pursued; not appropriate for the operator's scale.
  • ISO 27001. Same.
  • HIPAA BAA. SetForge is not designed for protected health information.
  • Off-site backup tier separate from primary hosting. Spaceship's backup retention is the only backup today.
  • 24/7 incident response. One operator; response within a few business days; urgent incidents communicated by email when outbound email is live.
  • Bug bounty program. Not run. Security reports welcome at hello@setforge.io; we'll credit and respond.
Why we publish this list: the things we don't have are as important to a buyer as the things we do. SetForge is appropriate for individual coaches, masters groups, school programs, and small clubs — not for medical-grade environments or organizations that require enterprise-tier certifications.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email hello@setforge.io with the details. We respond within a few business days. SetForge does not pay bug bounties today; we credit researchers in the changelog with their consent.
