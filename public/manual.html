User Manual

Everything you need to generate, customize, run, and track personalized swim workouts.

Overview

The Swim Workout Generator creates structured swim workouts tailored to your pool, pace, available equipment, and time budget. Every workout is built from four sections — Warm-Up, Drill, Main Set, and Cool-Down — chosen from a library of options calibrated for masters swimmers.

🎯
9 Workout Types
IM, Distance, Sprint, Endurance, Technique, Mixed, plus Backstroke, Breaststroke, and Butterfly specialty workouts.
🔁
Repeat Sets
Main sets can automatically repeat 2–3 rounds with rest between, filling your yardage target efficiently.
⏱
Pace-Aware Intervals
All intervals scale to your personal 100-yard pace target. Adjust globally or per set.
🏊
25yd & 50m Pools
Separate option banks for 25-yard and 50-meter pools. Native 50m sets for most workout types.
📱
Run Mode
Step through sets one at a time with a full-screen pace clock, elapsed time, and auto-advance rest timer.
📊
History & Stats
Every saved workout is logged. Stats panel shows sessions, total yards, weekly rate, and type breakdown.
⭐
Favorites
Star your favorite main sets. They get extra weight in the generator and are pinned in your History.
☁️
Cloud Sync
Your workout history, favorites, and settings are stored in the cloud and sync across devices.

Quick Start

Sign in with Apple

Tap "Sign in with Apple" on the landing page. Your workouts and settings are tied to your Apple ID.

Choose a workout type

Select one of the nine type cards: IM, Distance, Sprint, Endurance, Technique, Mixed, or a stroke specialty.

Set your target distance

Drag the yardage slider or tap a time preset (~60 / ~90 / ~2hr). Enter your 100-yard pace to get accurate time estimates.

Choose equipment

Check off the gear you have today. The generator only uses sets compatible with what you've selected.

Generate

Tap 🏊 Generate Workout. Review the four sections. Hit regenerate on any section you want to swap out.

Run or save

Tap ▶ Run to step through sets in the pool, or Save to History to log it and print later.

Signing In

The app uses Sign in with Apple for authentication. Your Apple ID is never shared — only a permanent, anonymized identifier is used to link your workouts and settings.

Returning users

Just click Sign in with Apple on the gate screen. Your existing account is recognized by Apple ID; everything (history, favorites, settings) comes back exactly as you left it.

New users (invite required)

Access is invite-only. To join, you need an invite link from someone who already uses the app. Invite links look like:

https://veronicacassidy.com/api/auth/apple?invite=CODE

Click the invite link, sign in with Apple, and you're in. The invite is consumed on first use; the same link won't admit a second account (unless the inviter configured it for multiple uses). If you see an invite_invalid, invite_expired, or invite_exhausted message, ask the inviter for a fresh link.

Sessions

Once signed in, your session persists via a secure cookie for 30 days. You can see all your active sessions and sign out from individual devices via the 👤 Account menu — see Account & Sessions.

Yardage & Pace

The Yardage Slider

Drag the slider to set your target maximum distance. The generator fills to within ~400 yards of this target, so a 2400yd target typically produces a 2000–2400yd workout.

Time presets

Tap ~60 min, ~90 min, or ~2hr to auto-set the slider based on your pace. The preset is calculated from your pace input, so set your pace first for accurate estimates.

Custom range (your personal min & max)

Click the left tick label (the minimum) or right tick label (the maximum) on the slider to edit your personal bounds. For example, if you never swim more than 3000yd, set the max to 3000 and it will stay there across sessions. Your custom range is saved to your account.

💡
One swimmer, different ranges. Two users can have completely different slider ranges — one at 1900–2400yd, another at 2400–4000yd. Each person's range persists independently.

Pace Input

Enter your comfortable 100-yard pace (format: M:SS, e.g. 2:00) in the pace field. This does two things:

  • Scales all intervals — every generated interval is adjusted from the masters baseline (2:00/100) to your pace.
  • Powers the time presets — the ~60/~90/~2hr buttons calculate the right yardage for your pace.

You can also rescale intervals after generation from within any workout block. Your default pace is saved to your account.

Generating a Workout

Once a workout type is selected and the slider is set, tap 🏊 Generate Workout. The generator:

  1. Picks a warm-up that fits within the yardage budget.
  2. Picks a drill set compatible with your equipment and warm-up.
  3. Picks a main set (possibly as a 2× or 3× repeat) that fills the remaining budget to within ~400 yards of your target. Favorited sets get extra weight; repeat variants get extra weight to fill higher yardage targets.
  4. Picks a cool-down to close the workout.
  5. Scales all intervals to your pace setting.

The summary bar shows total yardage, estimated duration, and your target range. Tap 🔄 Generate New Workout at any time to replace everything.

Workout Sections

Every workout has four sections displayed as color-coded blocks:

SectionColorPurpose
Warm-Up■ Sky blueEasy aerobic activation, 400–800 yds
Drill / Pre-Main■ TealTechnique focus, stroke preparation
Main Set■ BlueThe primary training stimulus
Cool-Down■ SlateEasy recovery swim, 200–400 yds

Each block shows its name, total yardage, and a table of sets. The main set block also shows a ⭐ favorite button and an optional repeat indicator (e.g. ×2 rounds).

Intervals

Each set shows a target interval in the rightmost column (e.g. On 2:15). These are pre-scaled to your pace setting. You can adjust them two ways:

Rescale all intervals

In the pace field above the slider, update your pace and the app rescales every interval in the current workout proportionally. This is useful when you want to try a harder or easier version of the same workout.

Edit a single interval

Click any interval in the workout table to edit it inline. Type the new interval (e.g. 2:30 or :45), then press Enter or click away to commit. The estimated duration updates immediately.

💡
Sets without intervals show "No interval" — these are typically easy recovery swims. The estimated duration for these sets uses a 35 yd/min fallback.

Set Descriptions

The description column in each set row is click-to-edit. Click any description text to open an inline editor, make your changes, then press Enter or click away. Changes flow through to the print layout, history save, and Run Mode.

Descriptions reset when you generate or regenerate a section.

Set Swap

The ⇄ button appears on individual sets within a block. Tap it to see a shorter and longer equivalent set. Swapping rescales the interval proportionally and updates the block's total yardage.

Use set swap to fine-tune the balance within a section without regenerating the whole block.

Repeat Sets

When the yardage target is high or when the generator determines it's the best fit, the main set may be selected as a repeat block — the same set performed 2 or 3 rounds with rest in between.

Repeat blocks are labeled with a round count (e.g. ×2 rounds, 30s rest between) and display each round separately with "Round 1 / Round 2" headers.

Editing rest between rounds

Click the ── 30s rest ── row between rounds to edit the rest duration inline. Enter a number of seconds and press Enter. In Run Mode, the rest timer between rounds uses this value.

ℹ️
Repeat vs. regenerate. Regenerating a repeat-set block replaces the entire set (including the round structure) with a different option. Use set swap or description editing to tweak a repeat block in place.

Favorites

Tap the ⭐ button on any main set block to mark it as a favorite. Favorited sets:

  • Get 3× extra weight in the generator's pick pool — you'll see them more often.
  • Appear in a dedicated Favorites panel in the History view.
  • Are stored per-user in the cloud and sync across devices.

Tap the ⭐ again to remove a favorite. Unfavoriting takes effect immediately in both the generator and the history panel.

Regenerating Sections

Each workout block has a ↺ regenerate button. Tapping it replaces only that section with a different option while keeping the other three sections unchanged.

The generator ensures the new section:

  • Has a different fingerprint from the current section (no immediate repeats).
  • Fits the remaining yardage budget.
  • Satisfies your equipment selection.
  • Gets its intervals scaled to your current pace.

If no valid alternative exists (e.g., the constraints are too tight), an inline error message appears explaining why. Try adjusting the max yardage or equipment selection.

⚠️
Regenerating clears the save status. Even if the workout was previously saved, a section regen marks it as unsaved. You'll be prompted to save again before printing.

Run Mode

Tap ▶ Run in the summary bar to enter Run Mode — a full-screen, section-by-section view designed for use at the pool deck.

Rest timer picker

Before the workout starts, choose your rest-between-sets preference:

OptionBehavior
ManualWaits for you to tap "Next Set" — you control the pace
0sFlashes "Done" for 1.5s then auto-advances
5s / 10s / 15sCountdown timer auto-advances to the next set

Section navigation

Run Mode steps through sets one at a time. The section name (Warm-Up, Drill, etc.) is shown in the top bar. Tap Next Set to advance, or use the back arrow to return to the previous set. When a section ends, you'll see a section summary before moving to the next.

Landscape mode

Rotate your device to landscape orientation for a full-screen Pace Clock view — the interval timer fills the screen. The section name appears in the top bar so you always know where you are in the workout.

Pace Clock

Tap any set row in Run Mode to open the Pace Clock — a large countdown timer for that set's interval.

ElementDescription
Countdown ringVisual arc depletes as the interval counts down. Green while running, fills amber near zero.
Elapsed timeShown in the top-right corner — total time since the workout started.
Set detailsReps × distance and description shown below the timer.
▶ / ⏸ buttonStart and pause the clock. Tap to begin on first open.
↺ ResetResets the clock to the full interval without leaving the set.

When the interval finishes, the clock flashes and (if you have a rest timer set) begins the between-set rest countdown automatically before advancing.

💡
Sets with no interval open a simplified view showing the set details without a countdown. Tap "Done" to advance.

Rest Timer

The rest timer controls how the app advances between sets in Run Mode. You choose the mode before starting (see Run Mode). In timed modes, a rest countdown appears on the pace clock screen after each set finishes — you can always tap Skip to advance early.

For repeat-set blocks, the rest between rounds uses the per-block rest value you set in the workout view (default 30s), regardless of your global rest timer setting.

Saving a Workout

Below the workout blocks, a Save to History form lets you log the workout:

FieldNotes
DateDefaults to today. Change it to log a past or future workout.
InitialsUp to 4 characters — used to filter history by person. Saved as your default for next time.
NotesFree-form text. Appears in the history entry and the print header.

Tap Save. The entry is added to your history immediately (optimistically) and synced to the cloud. The save button turns to a "Saved ✓" confirmation.

ℹ️
Planned workouts. Saving with a future date marks the entry as "Planned" rather than "Completed." You can toggle this status from the History view.

Tap 📜 History in the header to switch to the History view. It shows all your logged workouts, newest first.

  • Who — filter by initials / user if multiple people share the app.
  • All time / Last 4 weeks — scope the stats panel (see below).

Copy & Print

📋 Copy Text

Tap 📋 Copy Text in the summary bar to copy the entire workout as plain text to your clipboard. The format is human-readable and suitable for texting to a lane partner or pasting into Notes. The button flashes green for 2 seconds to confirm the copy.

Example output:

Distance Swim Workout — 2,400 yds (~68 min)
Pace target: 2:00/100

WARM-UP  (600 yds)
  4 × 100 — Easy freestyle, descend effort @ 2:15

MAIN SET — DISTANCE 100S REPEAT UNIT  (1,200 yds)  ×3 rounds, 30s rest between
  — Round 1 —
  4 × 100 — Steady descend, last 25 fast @ 2:00
  (30s rest)
  — Round 2 —  ...

🖨 Print Workout

Tap 🖨 Print Workout to open the system print dialog. The app renders a clean black-and-white layout optimized for 8.5×11 paper — the on-screen chrome disappears and only the workout content is printed.

If the workout hasn't been saved yet, you'll be asked whether to Save & Print or Print Without Saving.

💡
Printing from history. Tap 🖨 Print on any history entry to load that workout and print it directly.

Account & Sessions

Click the 👤 button in the top-right of the header to open the Account modal. It shows:

Identity

  • Initials & email — your identifying info. Email comes from Apple at sign-in; initials are set when you save your first workout (and editable thereafter).
  • Member since — when your account was created.
  • Last login — when you most recently signed in to any session.

Workouts summary

Total count across all pool modes, plus a breakdown by mode (SCY, SCM, LCM) showing count and total distance in the right unit.

Active sessions

One row per device/browser where you're currently signed in. Each row shows the browser/OS string, IP address, and last-seen timestamp. Your current device is highlighted blue with a ● this device badge.

  • Per-row Revoke — every non-current session has a Revoke button. Click it to immediately sign out that device on its next API request.
  • Sign out everywhere else — single button (top-right of the section, only visible when you have more than one session) revokes every session except the one you're currently using.
🔐
Lost a device? Sign in from any other browser, open the Account modal, and revoke the missing session. The cookie on that device becomes invalid within one API call.

Admin (admins only)

If your account has the admin flag set, a purple 🛡 Admin button appears in the header next to the 👤 button. Clicking it switches to the admin view, which has three tabs:

Users

Table of every user with their initials, name, email, workout count, last login, and any flags (admin / disabled). Per-row actions:

  • Edit — open a modal to update initials, full name, or email. Server-validates email format and length limits.
  • Disable / Enable — toggles is_disabled. A disabled user gets 403 on every authenticated request within one call (sessions stay in the DB but the per-request check fails).
  • Delete — confirms, then permanently deletes the user. Cascade-wipes their workouts, favorites, settings, and sessions. Audit events are preserved but anonymized. NOT REVERSIBLE.

Self-protection: you cannot disable or delete your own account from this view.

Invites

Create new invite codes and see the status of existing ones.

  • Create form — note (optional, e.g. "for Sarah"), max uses (default 1), optional expiry date. The generated code is shown in the row that appears.
  • Copy link — copies the share URL (https://veronicacassidy.com/api/auth/apple?invite=CODE) to your clipboard. Button flashes ✓ Copied! briefly.
  • Delete — removes the invite immediately.
  • Status badges — active, exhausted (all uses consumed), or expired (past the expiry date).

Audit log

Most-recent 200 security-relevant events in reverse chronological order. Each row shows the timestamp, event type, user prefix, IP, and a snippet of the details JSON. Filters:

  • event_type — exact match (e.g. auth.login.success, admin.user.disable, rate_limit.hit).
  • user_sub — full Apple sub. Useful when investigating a specific user's activity.
  • Clear button to reset filters.

Event categories you'll see: auth.* (login/logout/signup/reject/disabled_attempt), admin.* (user.disable/enable/delete/update, invite.create/delete, access_denied), invite.consume, workout.delete, csrf.reject, rate_limit.hit.

Tips & Tricks

Getting repeat sets to appear

Repeat sets are more likely at higher yardage targets (2400yd+). They get 2× weight in the pick pool. If you want repeats specifically, generate at 2400–3000yd and hit regenerate on the main set a few times until one appears.

Building a consistent routine

Star your favorite main sets. Over time, the generator will favor them — you get the variety of randomized warm-ups, drills, and cool-downs, but your main set stays in familiar territory.

Customizing before the pool

Generate at home, then use per-set interval editing and description editing to dial in exactly what you want. Copy to clipboard and text it to your lane partner, then save before you leave.

50m pool tips

Switch to 50m mode before generating. The slider will shift to 2000–4000m range. For IM or stroke specialty workouts, you'll see a notice that distances are in yards — these types don't yet have native 50m options.

Tracking multiple swimmers

Each Apple ID gets its own workout history, favorites, and settings. Use the Initials field on each save to identify who logged which workout — the History "Who" filter will then let you view each person's workouts separately.

Sharing a workout

The fastest path: generate → 📋 Copy Text → paste into Messages or Notes. For a nicer format: generate → save → 🖨 Print → save as PDF from the print dialog.
