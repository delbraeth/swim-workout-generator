What's not on the price list

Supporter tier Folded into a tip jar. If you'd like to support SetForge without needing the Coach tier, a "buy me a coffee" link is coming. No feature unlock; just a way to say thanks.
Ads · trackers · analytics SetForge runs no analytics (Google Analytics, Mixpanel, etc.), no ad pixels, no behavioral tracking, no session replay. Full sub-processor list.
Email + password sign-in Apple OAuth today; Google OAuth coming. We never store passwords because we never have them.

For boards and treasurers

If you're evaluating SetForge for a 501(c)(3) club or school program, the Program tier ships with a vendor paper kit:

  • Signed services agreement + Data Processing Addendum
  • EIN-bearing invoice (Stripe Invoicing) — quoted as annual contract ($300/yr)
  • Sub-processor list — already public at setforge.io/sub-processors
  • Breach-notification SLA (24 hours) — see setforge.io/security
  • Written continuity commitment — see next section
  • W-9 form with Competition Aquatics, LLC's EIN, for your accounts-payable file

The kit is sent on request to your treasurer or board. Email hello@competitionaquatics.com with your organization name and we'll attach the bundle.

Continuity commitment

SetForge is operated by a single founder. We take the single-operator risk seriously and have committed in writing — in our Terms of Service — to the following if SetForge ever ceases operation:

  • 90-day advance notice to all active users by email before service ends
  • Self-serve data export in JSON format, available throughout the notice period
  • Published migration scripts released to a public repository so any third party can self-host
  • Open-source the SetForge codebase at the start of the notice period, so the product can continue independently of the operator

This is the strongest continuity commitment a solo-operator SaaS can credibly make. We chose it on purpose.
