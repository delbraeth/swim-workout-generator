Changelog

Curated highlights from the SetForge build log — not every commit, just the things that change how the app works for you.

For a feature-by-feature view of how to USE these things, see the manual. For what's coming, see What's Coming.

2026

May 25 ugc-coach-sets-v1
Coach-authored sets (UGC) v1 — closed and tagged

Coaches can author their own bank sets via the 📝 My Sets view, snapshot any generated block to save it, share with their team, submit publicly for admin review, and graduate approved options into the canonical JS bank. Multi-tag means one set can land in multiple type/stroke pools without duplication (e.g., a kick set can live in distance, sprint, and endurance simultaneously).

May 23 reporting-v1-complete
Reporting v1 — six reports across three audiences

📊 Reports tab with Programming Mix (R1), Schedule Adherence (R2), Curation Log (R3), Program Recap for solo/masters (R4), Platform Health (R5 admin), and Curation Support (R6 admin). Print + PDF + Markdown export.

May 22 multi-lane-generate-v1 · n6-multipace-export-2026-05-19
Multi-lane generate + per-lane print

Generate one workout that fits every lane's pace, then auto-route to a print view with one page per lane. Yellow banner appears when the generator had to fall back from a strict lane fit.

May 22 disfavor-flag-v1 → set-disfavor-v1 → coach-disfavor-prop-v1 → hard-exclude-v1
Tri-state favorite / disfavor system

Every label, set, and engine template gets a three-way toggle: ★ Favorite (3× weight) · — Neutral · 👎 Disfavor (0.25× weight or hard-exclude). Coach picks propagate to swimmers in their groups. Symmetric audit panels under Profile show what you've curated.

May 21 template-engine-v1.1
Template engine + Bank/Engine/Mix toggle

Per-section toggle lets you choose how SetForge composes each block: from the canonical bank (curated), from the engine (generative), or mix (some of each). 17 templates today; ~3.9% fallback rate. Built-in anti-repeat memory so you don't get the same set every Tuesday.

May 23 run-screen-v1
Pace clock UI rebuilt for landscape phones

Larger clock (128px), reorganized hierarchy, INTERVAL/REST/GO labels, optional Lap button for split capture. Designed for the wall during a set — readable from across the pool.

May 17 relationships-stage4-rg-pilot-ready
Teams · groups · managed swimmers · "Assigned to me"

Full multi-stakeholder data model: coaches own teams, teams have groups, groups have swimmer members (account-holders OR coach-managed). Coaches generate workouts for a group; swimmers see them in 📥 Assigned to me and mark complete from their own phone.

May 20 setforge-rebrand-2026-05-20
SetForge brand + setforge.io launch

Product renamed to SetForge. New domain, new wordmark, new dark-mode palette, new icon set across all surfaces.

May 19 (pricing direction locked)
Pricing model decided

Coaches pay, swimmers free forever. Free / Coach $10 / Lesson (planned) / Program $25. Implementation triggers on the first paying pilot — see Pricing.

May 14 code-quality-complete
Code-quality sprint — five sessions, 33 punch-list items

Cleanup pass across the engine, picker, generator, history, and audit. No new features; meaningful boost to maintainability and a few quiet bug fixes.
