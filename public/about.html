About SetForge

SetForge is built by one swim official, in public, with no investors and no ads. This page is for the people who want to know who's on the other end before they sign up.

Patrick Cassidy
Founder · Operator · Official

I've spent more than two decades on deck as a USA, YMCA, High School, and US Paralympic swimming official, starting out in the YMCA 28 years ago. Along the way, I've worked everything from high school dual meets to state championships, served as Secretary for the Greater Dayton Swim & Dive Officials, and managed sectionals and league meets across the GWOC, Southwest Buckeye, and Miami Valley.

What pushed me to build this was simple: the systems coaches rely on don't match the reality of how practices actually run. Too much friction, not enough clarity, plus my wife made me.

So I built something better—and I'm the one who answers the email.

Why SetForge exists

Most swim coaches programming workouts for a small group fall into the same pattern: write the same warm-up week after week, scribble the main set on a whiteboard ten minutes before practice, lose the paper, never quite have time to vary it. The good workout-bank tools are built for big clubs with deep budgets. The simple tools don't generate workouts — they store them.

SetForge is the thing in between: a workout generator that respects how an actual coach programs (per-lane paces, mid-practice swaps, "no fly for Linda this week"), shared with the swimmers in your group at no cost to them, with the operational honesty that you wish vendors had.

What's honest about it

  • No analytics. No Google Analytics, Mixpanel, Hotjar, or anything like them. Full vendor list.
  • No ads, no tracking pixels, no behavioral profiling. Your data isn't a product.
  • OAuth-only auth. We can't lose your password because we never had it.
  • ToS-bound free-tier permanence. The features that are free today stay free forever. Written into our Terms of Service, not just a marketing promise.
  • Continuity commitment. If SetForge ever winds down: 90-day notice + self-serve data export + published migration scripts + the entire codebase goes open-source. Detailed in Pricing.
  • One person answers the email. That's the operator (founder, above). Replies typically within a few business days. There is no support team.

What's not (yet) on the website

SetForge is in the "good product, immature surface" phase. The engine is mature; the chrome around it is being built out month by month. If you're reading this and something on the marketing pages feels half-built — that's because it is. The plan for fixing it lives in the public phased plan (link will be live once the repo is public per the open-source-on-shutdown commitment).

How SetForge stays small on purpose

The market for "best swim workout tool for the working coach of 1–30 swimmers" is small enough that SetForge can serve it well without becoming a different kind of company. We're not trying to replace TeamUnify (club ops) or TrainingPeaks (multi-sport coaching) or MySwimPro (consumer fitness). We've explicitly conceded those markets so SetForge stays focused on the coach-on-deck-with-a-phone center of gravity. Some segments — Summer-League booster programs, triathletes who need Garmin integration, USA-S clubs that need full periodization — SetForge intentionally does not serve.

Who's behind the LLC
